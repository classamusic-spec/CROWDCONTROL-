How to Play

Every character faces a direction. Tap one to send it off the board — but only when every tile between it and its edge is empty.

Tap a blocked character and it shakes (that's a mistake). Clear the character in its way first — one move opens another.

Clear the whole crowd to win. Fewer mistakes and faster times earn more ⭐.
