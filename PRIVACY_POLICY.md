# Privacy Policy — Crowd Control Daily

**Effective date:** 2026

Crowd Control Daily is designed to respect your privacy. In plain terms: the
game keeps to itself.

## What we do not do

- **No account required.** You can play without signing up or logging in.
- **No location collection.** The app does not request or collect your location.
- **No contacts access.** The app does not read or access your contacts.
- **No tracking SDKs.** There are no analytics or third-party tracking libraries.
- **No advertising identifier.** The app does not use the device advertising ID,
  and there are no ads.
- **No personal data transmitted or sold.** We do not send your data anywhere,
  and we never sell it.

## Where your data lives

All progress — levels completed, stars, streaks, settings, cosmetics, and
statistics — is stored **locally on your device** using the app's on-device
storage (AsyncStorage). It stays on your device.

Because this data is local, **removing the app may remove your progress**.

## Offline by design

The game works **fully offline**. No internet connection is required to play, and
the app does not depend on any online service.
