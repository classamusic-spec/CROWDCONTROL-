# Store Listing — Crowd Control Daily

## Name

## Subtitle

Clear the crowd. Find the right way out.

## Short description

Tap to clear the crowd — a calm, offline logic puzzle. No ads, no IAP. (76)

## Long description

Every character in the crowd is facing a direction, and each one can only leave
when the path to that edge is clear. Tap them in the right order to empty the
board. Simple to learn, satisfying to solve — one tap at a time.

Come back every day for a fresh Daily Challenge: one deterministic puzzle for
everyone, with current-streak and longest-streak tracking so you can keep your
run alive. Finish clean and share your result — the share text never spoils the
solution.

Work through 60 handcrafted levels across three grid sizes, every one validated
by the built-in solver so it always has a fair solution. Earn stars to unlock
six themed environments — concert venue, airport, subway, theme park, school,
and shopping mall — plus characters, outfits, and effects, all earned by
playing.

Fully offline. No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no data collection.
Designed to be accessible and comfortable for one-handed play, with clear arrows
that read by shape (not color alone), large touch targets, high contrast, and a
reduced-motion option.
